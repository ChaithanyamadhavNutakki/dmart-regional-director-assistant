# Executive Summary

**Dataset:** DMart synthetic store performance data
**Period:** 1 April–29 June 2026
**Author:** Nutakki Chaithanyamadhav

## Headline Finding
Pune has the lowest mean daily revenue, but the evidence points to a footfall gap rather than a stockout gap.

## Magnitude
Pune's mean daily revenue is ₹636,358.55, 26.14% below the all-store mean of ₹861,535.24. Its mean footfall is 1,517.71, 25.00% below the all-store mean of 2,023.59. Revenue per footfall is ₹419.29 in Pune versus ₹425.75 overall, just 1.52% lower.

## What Is Driving the Gap—and What Is Not
Lower footfall is the leading explanation supported by the comparisons. Pune does not have the highest mean stockout hours, so the premise that it is the lowest-revenue store because of unusually high stockouts is not supported by this analysis.

## Supporting Facts
- Pune records 70 Poor-performance days; all stores combined record 71.
- Pune averages 2.32 stockout hours; Hyderabad averages the highest at 3.85.
- Revenue–footfall correlation across cleaned observations is 0.918.

## Open Hypotheses
**Unconfirmed:** Pune's lower footfall may reflect catchment or local competition. Test using location-level traffic and competitor data. **Unconfirmed:** SKU availability may affect conversion despite average stockout hours. Test using SKU-level stockouts, hourly sales and lost-sales data. Correlation does not establish causation.

## Recommendation
The **Regional Operations Head** should complete a Pune store diagnostic within **14 days**, comparing traffic, conversion, local competition and SKU availability with Mumbai and Bengaluru before approving targeted interventions.
